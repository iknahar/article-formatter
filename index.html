Title

The headline of your story, exactly as it should appear on your published page.

2

Subtitle

The line shown under the title.

3

Body copy

Paste the whole thing, SEO suite and all — parsing automatically starts right after a line reading "the body" or "the article", wherever that falls. Markers like **[Place Diagram 2 → folder/file.png]**, **[Place Image 9 → AI generated, prompt 9 at the end]** and **[Place Image 3 → external, search "keyword"]** are detected automatically (the number is optional), along with their *Caption →* and *Alt →* lines and the **Prompt N** paragraphs at the end.

4

Diagram folder

Select the folder that holds your diagram PNG/JPG/WEBP files. They're matched to the markers by filename.

5

AI & external images

For each AI image, copy the prompt into your generator, then click the 3:2 frame and paste the result (Ctrl+V). External images show the search keyword, find the image, copy it, and paste the same way. Drag & drop or click-to-browse also works.

Preview & edit

The text below is editable, click into any paragraph and fix what you want. Images carry their captions and alt tags. When it's right, hit Publish.

One click, fully automatic — Publish uploads the article and hands you back a live link right away. Only your 20 most recent published articles stay live; older ones are removed automatically. Save as Draft instead to keep working on it later — drafts are never limited or auto-deleted.
