Body copy

Paste the whole thing, SEO suite and all — parsing automatically starts right after a line reading "the body" or "the article", wherever that falls. Markers like **[Place Diagram 2 → folder/file.png]**, **[Place Image 9 → AI generated, prompt 9 at the end]** and **[Place Image 3 → external, search "keyword"]** are detected automatically (the number is optional), along with their *Caption →* and *Alt →* lines and the **Prompt N** paragraphs at the end.
